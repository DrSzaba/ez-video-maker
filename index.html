What it does

🎬

Make a Video

Put clips and photos together Movie-Maker style. Pick TikTok / Reels / Shorts (vertical) or YouTube (horizontal), 4:5 post or any custom size first and every clip fills the screen. Trim, split, rotate, crop, speed, 8 transitions with your own timing, 8 color styles, music, a big red button to record your voice, sound effects, titles, captions, scrolling credits and 4K upscale.

🔄

Convert Video

FLV, AVI, MOV, WMV, MKV, WEBM, MPG, 3GP and more → MP4 (or MOV, MKV, AVI, WEBM, WMV, GIF…). Pull the sound out of a video as MP3. Change the shape of an old horizontal video into a TikTok vertical one in a single click. Many files at once.

🎵

Convert Audio

MP3 ↔ WAV, plus M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG, WMA, AIFF and OPUS in any direction. Drop in a whole folder of songs and press one button. Your original files are never changed or deleted.

Record your voice

One big red RECORD button. A 3-2-1 countdown, pause and resume as often as you like, and the recording drops straight onto the timeline where your clip starts. Microphone only — never your camera or screen.

⇄

Transitions your way

Cut, Fade, Crossfade, Dissolve, Wipe, Slide, Zoom and Push between any two clips, each with its own length and direction. Or set one transition between all clips with a single click.

📐

Fills the screen

Clips fill the screen by default — no more black bars. New 4:5 Instagram / Facebook shape and a Custom size button. The preview shows a safe-area guide and where TikTok puts its caption and buttons, and a quick check before saving warns you about black bars, silent videos and blurry low-resolution clips.

How to install

  1. Click Download for Windows above and save the zip file.
  2. Right-click the zip → Extract All, then double-click EZ-Video-Maker-Setup.exe.
  3. Click Next, Install, Finish. Start it from the Desktop shortcut.
Seeing a blue "Windows protected your PC" box? That's Windows being careful with any new program from a small maker. Click More info, then Run anyway. Everything the program needs is inside the setup — nothing else to download or install.

Questions

Does it need the internet?

No. Making, converting and saving all happen on your own computer. The only time you need internet is to download the setup.

Does it work on a Mac or phone?

Not yet — it's for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch. No ads, no accounts, no watermark, no time limit. Donate only if you feel like it.

What format does it save?

MP4 (H.264), which TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and DistroKid all accept. Choose vertical 1080×1920 for TikTok, horizontal 1920×1080 for YouTube, 1080×1350 for a 4:5 post, or tick 4K upscale for 3840×2160 / 2160×3840.

Where do my voice recordings go?

Each recording is saved as a WAV in Videos\EZ Video Maker Recordings and placed on the Sound tab of your project, so you can move it, change its volume or reuse it later.

Something went wrong — how do I get help?

The program keeps a note of any problem at %LOCALAPPDATA%\EZVideoMaker\crash.log. Send that text and describe what you were doing.
